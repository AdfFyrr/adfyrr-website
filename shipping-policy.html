Shipping & Delivery Policy

How and when our services are delivered to you.

Last updated: 29 July 2026

AdFyrr — a sole proprietorship of Akansha Sharma, 18/1 Prakash Nagar, Dehradun, Uttarakhand 248001, India ("AdFyrr", "we", "us", or "our") — provides digital marketing services only. We do not sell, ship, or deliver any physical goods. This policy explains how our services and deliverables reach you and the timelines you can expect. It should be read together with our Terms of Service and Refund & Cancellation Policy.

1. No Physical Shipment

All of our offerings — strategy, advertising management, creative production, consulting, audits, and reports — are intangible services delivered electronically. No physical product is shipped and no courier, shipping charge, or delivery address is involved at any stage.

2. How Services Are Delivered

  • Email: onboarding documents, audits, reports, invoices, and creative files are sent to the email address you provide at the time of purchase;
  • Shared cloud folders: creative assets, ad copy, and working documents are shared via Google Drive, Notion, or a comparable platform;
  • Advertising platforms: campaign work is delivered directly inside your own Meta, Google, Shopify, Klaviyo, or equivalent accounts, to which you grant us access;
  • Scheduled calls: strategy calls, onboarding sessions, and reviews are delivered over Google Meet or Zoom, with the joining link sent by email.

Please ensure the email address and contact number you supply at checkout are accurate — they are the only channels through which we can deliver.

3. Delivery Timelines

  • Paid strategy / consultation call: a booking confirmation and scheduling link are sent by email immediately on successful payment (within 24 hours at the latest). The call itself takes place at the slot you select.
  • Monthly retainers: onboarding begins within 2 business days of payment confirmation and receipt of the required account accesses. Campaigns are typically live within 7–10 business days of onboarding being completed.
  • One-time audits and project work: delivered within the timeline stated in your proposal or Engagement Agreement, ordinarily 5–10 business days from payment and receipt of the necessary access.
  • Ongoing reporting: performance reports are shared on the cadence agreed in your Engagement Agreement (typically weekly or monthly).

Where a timeline depends on inputs from you — account access, brand assets, product information, or approvals — the clock starts once those inputs are received.

4. Delays

If delivery is delayed for any reason — including delays caused by advertising-platform review or account-verification processes, which are outside our control — we will inform you by email with a revised timeline. We do not charge any additional fee for a re-delivery caused by a delay on our side.

5. Non-Delivery or Delivery Issues

If you have paid for a service and have not received the corresponding confirmation, access, or deliverable within the timelines above, please contact us at hello@adfyrr.com or +91 90587 28587. We will investigate and either re-deliver or, where the service could not be delivered at all, process a refund in accordance with our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

6. Geographic Availability

Our services are delivered remotely and are available to clients across India and internationally. There are no delivery-area restrictions and no delivery charges.
